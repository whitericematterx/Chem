Explain the evidence

Metal: a lattice of positive ions is surrounded by mobile delocalized electrons, which carry charge through the structure.

Graphite: each carbon atom forms three covalent bonds, leaving one electron per carbon atom delocalized. These electrons can move along the layers and carry charge. The model here describes current along the layers; graphite resistivity depends on direction.

Fair-comparison assumption: use the same circuit and keep the other resistance contributions approximately constant. Changing the sample then changes total resistance mainly through its sample resistance. The model assumes a uniform, ohmic sample at constant temperature, with fixed source voltage and fixed contact resistance.

Try it: double L, then restore it and double A. Does doubling sample resistance halve current when the other resistance is non-zero?

Prediction: at fixed V and Rother, increasing ρ or L increases Rsample and reduces I. Increasing A reduces Rsample and increases I. The change in current depends on the total circuit resistance.
